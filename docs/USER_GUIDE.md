# EduPredict 3.0 — User Guide

## Student

1. Register and log in.
2. Review/update your profile.
3. Enter or update performance information.
4. Generate a prediction.
5. Review predicted score and risk level.
6. Read feature impacts and recommendations.
7. Review prediction history and trends.
8. Use the study plan and EduPredict Assistant.

## Teacher

1. Log in as a teacher.
2. Open the teacher dashboard.
3. Search/filter students.
4. Review performance and prediction information.
5. Identify students who may need support.
6. Review analytics and feature importance.
7. Record an intervention or follow-up note.
8. Export available student data when appropriate.

## EduPredict Assistant

The assistant can explain:
- System features and workflow.
- Student and teacher modules.
- AI and machine-learning concepts.
- Prediction, risk detection and explainability.
- Student-specific prediction information when the logged-in context provides it.

Example questions:
- What features does EduPredict have?
- Explain performance prediction.
- What is Random Forest?
- What is Gradient Boosting?
- What is my predicted score?
- Why am I at risk?
- What should I improve?
